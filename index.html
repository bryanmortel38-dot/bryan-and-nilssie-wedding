Our Story

Age 7 - The First Dance

We first met at a cotillion dance practice, unexpectedly paired together for a rehearsal.

Growing Up - Always Side by Side

We became neighbors, shared the same tutor, school service, high school, and youth organization. Somewhere along the way, we became best friends.

“Brotha” & “Sistah”

We knew each other’s stories, crushes, heartbreaks, and everything in between. We were inseparable but never in love.

Years Later - Something Changed

Life got busy and we drifted apart. Then the pandemic brought us close again, and this time, something felt different.

The Question

Bryan asked, “Pwede ba kitang ligawan?” Nilssie said no—at first. She was afraid to risk the friendship they had built for so many years.

And Then, Love

Slowly, our friendship blossomed into something more. We realized that perhaps we never really had to find each other, God had been placing us beside each other all along. On January 6, 2022, we officially began our journey as a couple.

August 30, 2026 - She Said Yes

After all those years of growing up side by side, Nilssie finally said yes to forever.

September 26, 2026 -

Forever Begins
♡

From a childhood dance partner to a lifelong partner. Our first dance brought us together. Today, we begin our forever.
